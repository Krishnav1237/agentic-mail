Overdue reasoning: its own group above Today, warm-neutral (dusty coral, ~16% border opacity) rather than red — enough to read instantly as "past due" without becoming an alarm. It carries full context because it's the most consequential tier, but has no gold — this particular item is routine late paperwork, not something Obligo specially caught, keeping overdue and gold visibly independent. Control area reasoning: title, open-count and overdue-count now read as one sentence-like unit instead of a heading+subheading+toolbar stack; search collapses to an icon, and Category/Priority/Due merge into a single "Filter ⌄" affordance that expands on demand — so the page opens looking finished, not configurable. Density reasoning: Overdue/Today rows get a reason-line (why + what's prepared + source); This Week keeps title+deadline+priority only; Later/No-deadline drop to one line with just a relative date or priority tag — attention earns detail, distance earns quiet.
